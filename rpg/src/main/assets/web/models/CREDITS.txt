3D models in this folder are converted (meshopt-compressed, merged) from the KayKit packs by
Kay Lousberg (https://kaylousberg.com), released under CC0 1.0 Universal:
  - KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers
  - KayKit Character Pack: Skeletons
  - KayKit Dungeon Remastered
  - KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack
tools/web3d/build-assets.mjs rebuilds them from the original packs.
